    How it works on the watch

    The watch's tap sensor only feels knocks, so TAP-3 uses counts and pauses, like the tap code POWs used through cell walls. A dash isn't possible. Wait for the buzz after the password, tap the symptom, pause a second, tap the severity. The watch buzzes your code back; a long buzz means nothing was logged.

    Timing matches Mr Morse: first two taps within 0.35 s, then a pause of 0.45–1.5 s before the third.

    Progress

    Start with two symptoms. The next one unlocks when each one you have is above 90% over its last 10 tries. Weak codes come up more often.

    Next review

    A short session of 10 spaced out over days beats one long cram. Pass a session (90%+) and the gap grows: 1, 2, 4, 7, then 14 days. Miss and it goes back to tomorrow.

    Wallet card

    Built from your setup, credit-card size (85.6 × 54 mm). To print it, open this demo from its file and use your browser's Print; only the cards print, at real size. Cut out, fold along the long edge, keep it with your cards.

    Use the card to look a code up when you're unsure, and practise so you stop needing it. Morse teachers found that reading from a chart builds a slow lookup habit.

    Your three symptoms
